  log-only signal.
- The D4 regression test called `applyScore('reject', ...)` directly with a
  hand-shaped argument, rather than driving the actual reject-button code

## Round-3 final re-verification: REJECT (one more timing race), and the fix

Re-dispatched the reviewer a third time. It confirmed the NEW-1 fix's
ordering was genuinely correct (capture precedes `replayDot`) and that
everything from rounds 1-2 held up, including a third independent hand-
derivation of clause B=31 / clause B2=68 at N=6. But it found **NEW-2**: the
round-2 fix only closed the REMOVAL-side race. `state.dots` is asynchronous
on BOTH edges -- `flyDot`'s animated settle lands a NEW dot in `state.dots`
~560ms after a paste, while `completeR1Paste`'s own advance timer fires at
400ms. On the 4th (final) round-1 paste, `state.round1Dots = state.dots.length`
was therefore still being read ~160ms BEFORE that paste's own dot had
finished landing -- under-reporting 3 instead of 4 in any real browser. The
round-2 harness's checks passed only because its zero-size
`getBoundingClientRect` stub forced `flyDot` down its instant-settle
fallback, entirely bypassing the 560ms delay -- the checks were validating
the STUB's behavior, not the game's. (This is the same class of blind spot
as D1's original "no exception" check, one layer down -- the reviewer noted
this explicitly.)

While investigating, it also surfaced a related, PRE-EXISTING bug (not
introduced by any of these fixes): `renderAIPane` rendered a manual
"continue" (`nextTurn`) affordance even in round 1 (`dragMode`), where
advancement is already automatic via `completeR1Paste`'s own timer. A click
on that button within the ~400ms window could double-advance `turnIndex` and
silently skip a round-1 card.

**Root cause, per the reviewer's own diagnosis**: `state.dots` is the FX
reservoir's live backing store, asynchronous in both directions -- no single
instant of sampling `state.dots.length` is ever safe for a correctness-
bearing tally. The fix must not read the array at all.

**Fixed**: `state.round1Dots++` now runs directly inside `completeR1Paste`,
at the same place `addDot` is called -- i.e. the tally increments AT THE
PASTE EVENT itself, making it correct by construction and completely
independent of any FX timing constant (560ms settle, 1900ms replay, or any
future retiming of either). The `state.dots.length`-based derivation was
deleted from `startR1Boomerang` entirely (only an explanatory comment
remains, pointing future readers at `completeR1Paste`). Also fixed the
double-advance race: the `nextTurn` button in `renderAIPane` is now gated on
`!dragMode`, since round 1 never needs a manual continue affordance at all.

New harness: `.omc/drafts/verify-r1-timing-race.js` -- uses a virtual clock
(a priority queue of `{fireAt, fn}` fired in real time order, honoring actual
`setTimeout` delays rather than draining them all at once) plus NON-zero
`getBoundingClientRect` stubs specifically so `flyDot` takes its real
animated path instead of the instant-settle fallback that hid NEW-2 from
every prior harness. Confirms `round1Dots` stays exactly 4 through a
realistically-timed full round-1 playthrough (checked immediately after the
4th paste, after `replayDot(1)` fires at +1900ms, and again at the very end),
and confirms the `nextTurn` affordance is entirely absent in round 1 so there
is nothing left to race against. All seven harnesses (six from rounds 1-2,
plus this one) pass clean after this fix.
